A GTA 6 meme posted on X: a Leonida Auction House sale record for
"Lot 2013 — ONE (1) PATIENCE". Thirteen years of the owner's bids climb
from $0.13 of allowance (2013) to his final $78.99 at Delay No. 2 — and
his own AI agent (Paddle #AI, bidding online from make.fun) outbids him
at $79.99, exactly the price of GTA VI. The make.fun ghost runs the sale
as auctioneer. The concept was built from a study of the dev
leaderboard's top 50 and verified original against all 4,531 ranked
entries (zero auction/bid/hammer entries in the field).

The post on X is the entry; `POST.json` names it and `main.ts` hands it to
the make.fun gate.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
